# Business Insights – Product Sales Analysis

## 1. Regional Sales Performance

North recorded the highest revenue at 967,957.90, while South had the lowest at 827,768.06. East, West, and Central generated relatively similar revenue.

The difference between North and South is approximately 140K. This shows that sales are not evenly distributed across regions. I would look into what is driving stronger sales in North and whether the lower-performing regions have differences in customer demand, product availability, or marketing activities.

## 2. Monthly Revenue Trends

March 2023 was the strongest month, generating 208,548.70 in revenue, while October 2023 recorded the lowest revenue at 78,445.70.

Looking at the yearly averages, monthly revenue increased from 141,489.17 in 2023 to 147,662.89 in 2024. It increased further to 151,694.63 during January–June 2025.

This indicates a gradual improvement in average monthly revenue. However, sales still fluctuate from month to month. I would look at seasonal patterns and identify what contributed to the unusually high and low months before making future sales forecasts.

## 3. Order Return Analysis

Out of 1,500 orders, 372 were returned, giving a return rate of 24.8%. This means almost one out of every four orders was returned.

This is one area I think the business should pay more attention to. A high number of returns can affect revenue and increase handling and delivery costs. The next step would be to identify which products have the most returns and understand the main reasons behind them.

## 4. Customer Type Analysis

Retail customers generated 2,195,524.80 in revenue, while Wholesale customers generated 2,184,467.00. The difference is only 11,057.80, meaning both customer groups contribute almost equally to total revenue.

There isn't a significant gap between these two segments. Rather than focusing on just one group, the business should maintain both customer bases. It would also be useful to compare their order frequency and average order value to understand which segment offers better opportunities for growth.

## 5. Payment Method Analysis

Online payments generated the highest revenue at 971,114.70, followed by Cash at 950,387.80. Credit Card generated 866,483.30, Gift Card generated 821,585.25, and Debit Card recorded the lowest revenue at 770,420.44.

Online and Cash payments are performing quite closely, while the other payment methods contribute comparatively less. I would not recommend discontinuing any payment method based on revenue alone. Instead, comparing the number of transactions and average transaction value for each method could help the business better understand customer payment patterns.

## 6. Product Performance Analysis

Tablet generated the highest revenue at 684,539.40, closely followed by Laptop at 684,417.00 and Printer at 684,387.40. Phone recorded the lowest revenue at 497,162.80.

One interesting finding is that Tablet, Laptop, and Printer generated almost the same revenue. Phone and Desk performed comparatively lower.

I would look further into the sales quantity and profit margin of each product before deciding which products deserve more attention. Higher revenue does not necessarily mean higher profitability, so this information would be useful when making inventory and pricing decisions.

## Overall Conclusion

This analysis helped me understand how different factors contribute to the company's sales performance.

A few things stood out. North generated the highest regional revenue, while Retail and Wholesale customers contributed almost equally to overall sales. Online payments generated the highest revenue among payment methods, and Tablet was the highest revenue-generating product.

The other finding I would pay more attention to is the 24.8% order return rate. Understanding why customers are returning products could help the business identify areas that need improvement.

Overall, this project provides a useful overview of sales performance and highlights areas where further analysis could support better business decisions, particularly around product returns, regional sales, and product profitability.
